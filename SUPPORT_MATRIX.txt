v0.28.13 support scope (implemented; still requires live testing)

FRIENDLY LOADOUTS
- Different vanilla ship hulls/layouts, hull capacities and weapon loadouts: allowed.
- Combat drones: laser and beam attacks use the host's shared-enemy damage authority.
- Defense / anti-combat drones: native local defense, no duplicated interception.
- System repair / anti-personnel drones: native local crew behavior.
- Hull repair / shield drones: local owner only.
- Multiple friendly artillery systems: allowed; friendly system state is not copied.
- No guns / drone-only / non-attacking support ship: allowed.
- Friendly cloaking and clone bays: already allowed as local systems in prior builds.
- Inventory, reactor allocation and enemy targeting of friendly ships remain independent.

LIMITS RETAINED
- Player teleporter, hacking and mind control: blocked until their cross-ship effects
  have explicit authoritative state and lifecycle support.
- Boarding drones and unknown drone types: blocked. They transfer/create crew across
  ships and cannot be fixed by merely forwarding a projectile.
- Enemy drone types: existing defense and combat support only. Enemy crew drones,
  hull repair and shield drones remain blocked by the existing validator.
- Enemy teleporters: host-only boarding from v0.28.8 remains experimental.
- Enemy cloaking, clone bays, mind control and multiple artillery remain blocked.
- Not every vanilla ship is supported yet: an unsupported installed/equipped system
  can still block combat, even when unpowered.
- Partner orbital drone bodies/positions are not replicated as a second drone object.
  The shared attack/damage is relayed; local friendly drones remain native objects.
- Multiverse/custom content is not validated.

BEFORE CALLING THIS 1.0
1. Validate supported mixed ships, simultaneous fire, drone types, reward chains,
   sector transitions and repeated fights across full runs on both host roles.
2. Add player boarding/teleport with explicit crew ownership, recall and cleanup.
3. Add authoritative hacking and mind-control effects and remaining enemy systems.
4. Cover remaining vanilla events: surrender/escape/quests/stores/crew death and
   multi-stage flagship outcomes without separate progression.
5. Validate save/continue/reconnect behavior and packaging on clean installations.
These are remaining work items, not features claimed by this patch.
